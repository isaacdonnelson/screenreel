---
name: screenreel
description: >
  Records a product on the Linux display that can click it and renders a
  polished demo on the Mac.
---

Follow the screenreel skill.

Use the screenreel MCP tools when they are connected. Otherwise run `python3 linux/screenreel` on the Linux display. Render and check the picture on the Mac.

Ask for the product URL, the click path, and a background hex before recording. Mark each step while you record.

Return the path to `out.mp4` and the step timeline. Do not launch Cursorful. Do not add voice, music, captions, or title cards.
